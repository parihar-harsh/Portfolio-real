04 / How I work

Understand. Build. Verify.

  1. 01

    Start with the problem

    Understand the user, constraints, data, and boundaries. Map the smallest useful workflow before choosing the implementation.

  2. 02

    Connect the pieces

    Build clear interfaces between the frontend, APIs, storage, and background work. Keep contracts and failure states explicit.

  3. 03

    Test the real journey

    Check validation, edge cases, accessibility, and deployment behavior. Document what works and what still needs verification.
